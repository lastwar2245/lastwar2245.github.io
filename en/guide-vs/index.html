🔁 Daily routine — EVERY DAY

Before spending an important resource, check Alliance Duel (VS), Arms Race, active events and current R4/R5 instructions. If a task, threshold or score changes, the live in-game panel always takes priority.

✅ Check every day

  • 🎯 Complete daily tasks and aim for the full threshold shown in game (recent guides often use 200 points as a reference).
  • 🎁 Claim free and capped rewards: idle rewards, login rewards, VIP chest and free shop chests when available.
  • 💎 If these bonuses are available in your game version, also claim the free diamonds from “Like” rewards in Storm Arena and 3v3.
  • 🏗️ Keep construction queues active unless a completion is deliberately held for the correct VS day.
  • 🔬 Keep useful research running and, when possible, time completion for a scoring day.
  • 🪖 Keep barracks / troop training active without burning speedups saved for Friday.
  • 🏛️ Before a major build, research or training push, check whether a useful Capitol / official position buff is available.
  • 🤝 Send alliance helps and make alliance donations EVERY DAY.
  • 🚂 If the Alliance Train is available: board it, choose a useful wagon and contribute trade contracts according to alliance instructions.
  • 📅 Check the event calendar, registrations and deadlines; read R4/R5 announcements before spending.
  • 🏅 Check Arms Race and align actions with the active phase. When practical, target the daily badge/chest milestones; 18 badges is a common community benchmark for the three daily chests, but always verify your live game panel.
  • 🔍 Check radar tasks without claiming banked tasks too early; also avoid sitting at the storage cap long enough to stop refresh progress.
  • 🕵️ Check secret tasks, use relevant attempts and help alliance members when the option is available.
  • 🚚 Check trade trucks and dispatch the best quality on the correct VS day; follow server 2245 anti-looting rules.
  • ⚔️ Use expiring daily attempts: Arena, 3v3, limited modes and active events; claim free bonuses where available.
  • ⚡ Avoid capped stamina; use free/daily refills when available and respect any cooldown between them.
  • 🧟 Complete useful daily PvE such as zombies, Doom Walker and rallies, according to the objectives shown in your daily tasks. As a benchmark, some current guides recommend 1 Doom Walker + up to 20 rallies when those objectives are active.
  • 🏆 Check available recurring events (Honorable Campaign, Alliance Exercise / Marshal, Wanted Bosses, etc.) and claim rewards before they expire.
  • 🌾 Before logging off, send spare squads to gather if they are not required for an event.
  • 📦 Keep resource chests unopened until you actually need the resources; inventory chests are safer than exposed base resources.
  • 🔴 Do not automatically tap every red dot: first check whether the reward can wait for a better VS / Arms Race phase.
  • 📢 Read alliance announcements before any operation, war or major teleport.
🚫 Some generic guides recommend daily raiding. For server 2245, our alliance rule takes priority: no looting on 2245.

🎯 Simple priority order

1. Anything that expires today: registrations, limited attempts, rewards or events. 2. Alliance actions: helps, donations, train, rallies and scheduled operations. 3. Production queues: construction, research and troops. 4. Map activity: radar, zombies, gathering and missions. 5. Strategic spending only at the right VS / Arms Race timing.

⚔️ Saturday — Enemy Buster

VS

Objective: Score combat points without giving away free points.

🕵️ Stealth attack — mine to mine

On Saturday, play more discreetly by moving a team from mine to mine or nearby points and striking at the last moment. The goal is to reduce exposure time, surprise the target and limit retaliation.

  • Move gradually toward the enemy area without staying still too long.
  • Keep an emergency shield or teleport.
  • Strike only profitable, coordinated targets.
  • Scout a mine or another base before attacking to trigger / use the frenzy bonus; useful for Marshal exercises and similar events too.
  • Re-shield as soon as possible, ideally at the end of frenzy, or return to the hive.
🥷 During frenzy — move somewhere safer

After attacking on the enemy server, avoid staying beside the target for the whole frenzy period.

  • Move to a quieter or less exposed corner of the enemy server.
  • If the base is burning, do not automatically extinguish it: monitor wall integrity and prioritize retreat and the next shield.
  • Remove teams / troops from the wall if you do not intend to defend, to limit losses.
  • Keep an emergency teleport.
  • As soon as frenzy ends: activate a shield and return neatly to the hive.
❄️ Event trick — temporary protection

If an instanced event such as Winter Storm is available and the game actually shows temporary protection for your base while you participate, it can provide a short waiting window during frenzy while also letting you benefit from the event.

  • Check the protection icon before entering: never assume the shield is guaranteed.
  • Use the event for a few minutes only if protection is visibly active.
  • On exit, if frenzy has ended: activate a real shield and return to the hive.

🪵 Lumberjack tips and tricks

Practical rules to progress faster, score better in VS and avoid costly mistakes.

⏳ Stock · 🎯 Claim on the right day · 🤝 Play for the alliance · 🛡️ Protect yourself · 📈 Prioritize T1

⏳ 1. Stock before clicking

  • Do not click red dots too quickly.
  • Finish some actions the day before, then claim them the next day if the VS theme matches.
  • Keep rewards that can wait: buildings, research, chests, shards and tickets.

🤝 2. Alliance donations — EVERY DAY

  • Donate EVERY DAY to unlock collective bonuses.
  • Prioritize technologies useful for VS, power and combat.
  • An alliance that donates progresses faster and scores better.

🔍 3. Radars: prepare the day before

  • Radars matter on days when “Complete 1 Radar Task” scores points.
  • Prepare mainly Sunday night for Monday, Tuesday night for Wednesday and Thursday night for Friday.
  • Watch the maximum radar-task limit.

🌾 4. Monday gathering

  • Start gathering Sunday evening.
  • Have it finish after 04:00.
  • Ideally claim after 04:05 to avoid counting issues.

🎁 5. Building gifts and rewards

  • Do not automatically open building-related gifts.
  • Save them for Tuesday or Friday when they can score VS points.
  • Apply the same logic to research, drone components, heroes and training.

🪖 6. Troops: staggered production

  • Restart barracks from Sunday to rebuild after VS / Capitol.
  • Produce all week without burning speedups.
  • Make the big push Friday: training + N5 → N6 → N7 → N8 → N9 → … promotions.

🦸 7. Heroes: levels of 10

  • Do not level heroes randomly.
  • Aim for useful milestones: 10 / 20 / 30 / 40.
  • Prioritize the main team and heroes used every day.

🧬 8. Teams: T1 first

  • One strong T1 is better than three average teams.
  • Defenders in front, attackers and supports in back.
  • Gear: armor/radar on frontline, cannon/chip on backline.

🛡️ 9. Shield before Enemy Buster

  • Friday night: shield before sleeping.
  • Cover at least until after 04:00 / 04:05.
  • If absent: shield until Saturday evening.

🏥 10. Heal in small batches

  • Heal batches of about 50 troops.
  • Ask for alliance help, restart, repeat.
  • Avoid locking the hospital during combat.

🧱 11. If you forgot your shield

  • Remove teams from the defense wall.
  • Goal: limit troop losses.
  • The enemy scores less and your base becomes less profitable to attack.

🕵️ 12. Stealth attack — mine to mine

  • Approach discreetly by moving from mine to mine or nearby point.
  • Strike a profitable target at the last moment.
  • Return under shield or to the hive immediately afterward.

🕵️ 13. Scouting & frenzy bonus

  • Before attacking, scout a mine or another base to trigger / use the frenzy bonus.
  • This can also help during Marshal exercises and similar events.
  • Then strike quickly and prepare a safer position while frenzy is still active.

🥷 14. After the attack: hide while frenzy is active

  • After attacking on the enemy server, do not stay beside the target: move to a quieter corner or a less exposed location.
  • If the base is burning, do not automatically extinguish the fire: watch wall integrity and prioritize safety, retreat and the next shield. Prolonged burning reduces integrity and can eventually teleport the base, so this is only a temporary tactic.
  • Remove teams / troops from the defense wall if you do not intend to defend: this greatly limits military losses if you are hit again.
  • Keep an emergency teleport and avoid standing still in a busy area.
  • As soon as frenzy ends: re-shield, then return neatly to the hive using available teleports.

❄️ 15. Temporary event: gain a protection window

  • Some instanced events, for example Winter Storm or similar battlefields, may move you into a separate event map for a few minutes.
  • If the game actually shows a temporary protection for your base while you participate, that window can help you wait for frenzy to expire while also earning event rewards.
  • Always check the shield / protection icon before relying on this: event rules can change with the event or an update.
  • On exit: if frenzy has ended, activate a real shield and return to the hive. Two birds with one log. 🪵🛡️

🚫 16. Server 2245 rule

  • On 2245: loot nothing.
  • No trucks, tasks, gathering, bases or resources.
  • Targets are VS opponents or enemy servers.

🏠 17. Return to the hive

  • Do not stay exposed after an attack.
  • Use free VS teleports to return cleanly.
  • Re-shield as soon as possible, ideally at the end of attack frenzy.
  • Back home, park neatly in the hive. 🏠🪵

📢 18. Alliance communication

  • Warn the alliance before attacks.
  • Share your position on the enemy server.
  • Coordinate attackers, garrisons, scouting and absent players.

🧠 19. Final lumberjack rule

  • Prepare the day before.
  • Claim on the right day.
  • Do not waste.
  • Do not give away free points.

🎯 20. Advanced VS optimisation

  • Prioritise Alliance Duel research with Valor Badges when appropriate; it compounds future VS scoring.
  • Align major actions with Arms Race phases. Recent Arms Race guides use 18 badges for the top daily chest; always verify the live event panel.
  • Manage radar storage without sitting at the cap for too long.
  • Use waterfall training on Friday: stagger barracks and promote through N5 → N6 → N7 → N8 → N9 → …
  • Aim for personal chests first, then spend further only when the alliance result needs it. Recent guides often show 7.2M for the final VS chest when unlocked; check your live thresholds.
  • Check Capitol buffs before major build/research/training pushes.
  • Keep resource chests closed until needed.
  • On Saturday, secure non-combat points (UR trucks, Legendary secret tasks, counted speedups) before taking PvP risks, scout targets, and prioritise the rival VS alliance when its multiplier is active.
  • Monday exception: Hero EXP may score on Day 1; use only a controlled amount for Arms Race/chests and save the bulk hero push for Thursday.

⚔️ Saturday — Enemy Buster

Objective: Score combat points without giving away free points.

🕵️ Stealth attack — mine to mine

On Saturday, play more discreetly by moving a team from mine to mine or nearby points and striking at the last moment. The goal is to reduce exposure time, surprise the target and limit retaliation.

  • Move gradually toward the enemy area without staying still too long.
  • Keep an emergency shield or teleport.
  • Strike only profitable, coordinated targets.
  • Scout a mine or another base before attacking to trigger / use the frenzy bonus; useful for Marshal exercises and similar events too.
  • Re-shield as soon as possible, ideally at the end of frenzy, or return to the hive.

🥷 During frenzy — move somewhere safer

After attacking on the enemy server, avoid staying beside the target for the whole frenzy period.

  • Move to a quieter or less exposed corner of the enemy server.
  • If the base is burning, do not automatically extinguish it: monitor wall integrity and prioritize retreat and the next shield.
  • Remove teams / troops from the wall if you do not intend to defend, to limit losses.
  • Keep an emergency teleport.
  • As soon as frenzy ends: activate a shield and return neatly to the hive.

❄️ Event trick — temporary protection

If an instanced event such as Winter Storm is available and the game actually shows temporary protection for your base while you participate, it can provide a short waiting window during frenzy while also letting you benefit from the event.

  • Check the protection icon before entering: never assume the shield is guaranteed.
  • Use the event for a few minutes only if protection is visibly active.
  • On exit, if frenzy has ended: activate a real shield and return to the hive.
