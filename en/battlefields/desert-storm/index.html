🌪️ Desert Storm

Desert Storm is an alliance battlefield where victory comes mainly from objective control, rotations and coordination rather than individual fight count.

📅 Before battle

  • Monday → Wednesday: request participation and select your available time slots.
  • R4/R5 choose starters and substitutes; requesting does not guarantee a slot.
  • Thursday: check matchmaking, group, role and battle time.
  • Friday: be ready before the slot and read alliance orders.

🏛️ Battlefield priorities

1. Capture starting objectives, especially Oil Refineries. 2. Secure enough Field Hospitals to sustain the team. 3. Use the Info Center and Science Hub for score and teleport advantages. 4. When central objectives unlock, concentrate top players on the Nuclear Silo; Arsenal and Mercenary Factory are tactical multipliers. 5. Wells/boxes are mainly for individual points when the battlefield situation allows it.

🎯 LaB simple rules

  • Follow R4/R5 orders and alliance score priorities.
  • Avoid solo fights that pull you away from objectives.
  • If your troops are exhausted or a substitute needs the slot, leave the battlefield properly.
  • Exact scoring and schedules can change: verify the event panel on battle day.
