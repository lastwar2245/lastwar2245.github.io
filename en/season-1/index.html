Season 1

🦠 Season 1 — The Crimson Plague

Season 1 adds a full progression layer: Virus Resistance, seasonal buildings, professions, Genetic Recombination, Exclusive Weapons, Stronghold/City conquest and cross-warzone warfare.

Calendar: recent community guides usually describe 2 weeks of Pre-Season + 8 active Season 1 weeks + a Celebration phase. Exact timers, declaration days and some objectives can vary by server wave: the in-game countdown and rules always take priority.

🎯 Five priorities for the whole season

  • Raise Virus Resistance quickly enough to avoid being blocked by infected zombies, Doom Elites and contaminated areas.
  • Develop Protein Farms and the Virus Research Institute without scattering seasonal resources.
  • Follow the R4/R5 territory plan for Strongholds, Cities, Outposts and cross-warzone wars.
  • Prepare Kimberly, DVA and Tesla Exclusive Weapons: 5★ hero plus the right weapon shards.
  • Keep shields, healing, speedups and hospital capacity for war weeks; do not trade huge troop losses for small Influence gains.

📦 Pre-Season — two preparation weeks

  • Open the Season 1 timeline and review rules, rewards and warzone grouping.
  • R4/R5 should identify strong neighbors and possible Stronghold/City routes early.
  • Stock shields, healing speedups, general speedups and troops.
  • Do not burn every resource right before launch; the opening days are demanding.
  • A Capitol Conquest may still happen during Pre-Season, so follow your server calendar.

Alliance goal: enter Day 1 with a territory route, rally leaders, shield policy and clear communications.

🧪 Week 1 — establish the virus loop

First priorities

  • Build and upgrade the Virus Research Institute immediately.
  • Develop Protein Farms and collect Immune Protein.
  • Hunt manageable zombies for Mutant Crystals.
  • Do not force infected targets far above your resistance level.
  • Join the first Military Stronghold and City captures according to the alliance route.

Genetic Recombination

  • Collect all 6 Gene Fragment types.
  • Recombine a complete set for rewards.
  • Trade duplicates with alliance members instead of remaining blocked by one missing fragment.

Kimberly Exclusive Weapon

  • Kimberly must be 5★.
  • Activation uses 50 Kimberly Exclusive Weapon shards; Universal EW shards are used after activation.
  • The associated Battle Pass is time-limited, so check the countdown as soon as it appears.

Prepare for Week 2: 5★ Mason, healing stock, stable Virus Resistance and the next territory route.

🛡️ Week 2 — Mason UR, Crimson Legion and Lv.3–4 Cities

Mason promotion

  • The event enables Mason SSR → UR promotion under the requirements shown in-game.
  • Check stars, Wall of Honor, shard cost and squad impact before confirming.

Level Swap EXP

  • Level Swap EXP swaps the EXP level of two heroes, not their star rank.
  • It is useful for quickly moving EXP to a new priority hero.

Crimson Legion

  • The Crimson Legion attacks Alliance Cities in waves.
  • R4/R5 should announce the target City, timing, reinforcement slots and strongest squads.
  • Members should reinforce the requested target instead of spreading power everywhere.
  • Better wave survival means better rewards; failed defense can cost the City.

Expansion

  • Push toward Level 3 and Level 4 Cities.
  • Keep Virus Resistance rising in parallel.

✈️ Week 3 — Military Bases, expeditions and DVA

Troop-type boosts

  • Unlock seasonal Tank / Missile / Aircraft Military Bases.
  • They consume Immune Protein + Mutant Crystals and buff the matching hero type.
  • Prioritize your main squad type instead of leveling all three evenly when resources are tight.

Warzone Expedition

  • R4/R5 can place Alliance Outposts in other warzones.
  • Outposts become launch points for foreign Stronghold and City captures.
  • Cross-warzone contaminated land carries more PvP risk: shield and pull troops back before long absences.
  • Read the exact war-day schedule in your server event screen.

Lv.5–6 Cities

  • Expansion advances toward Level 5 and 6 Cities, which are key prerequisites for the next phase.

DVA Exclusive Weapon

  • DVA must be 5★.
  • Activation: 50 DVA EW shards, then Universal EW shards for upgrades.
  • Avoid spreading Universal EW shards across several weapons unless you deliberately need small unlocks.

⚔️ Week 4 — declarations, Warzone Duel and Capitol

  • Real cross-warzone warfare begins: Cities and Strongholds become Influence tools.
  • Older guides describe dedicated declaration days, but schedules have changed across server generations; always verify the event screen.
  • Warzone Duel pits servers against each other based on Influence rankings.
  • City of Apocalypse and the Capitol become major objectives.
  • Secure territorial prerequisites before battle; raw power is useless if your alliance cannot legally connect to the target.

Pre-war checklist

  • Hospital capacity available.
  • Shields ready.
  • Troops healed and formations saved.
  • Alliance/personal teleports available.
  • Targets and timing communicated by R4/R5.
  • No improvised attack that breaks the territory route.

🏛️ Week 5 — Infinite Octagon

  • Infinite Octagon drives warzones to fight over Capitols in other servers.
  • Convert captures into Influence without exhausting the army before later rounds.
  • Scouting, rallies, occupation and defense should be treated as one operation.
  • Lower-power players still add major value through reinforcements, healing, teleports, intel and disciplined execution.

Common mistake: losing huge numbers of troops for a target that barely changes the Influence ranking.

🔧 Week 6 — Warm-up and Tesla Exclusive Weapon

  • Usually a lighter week: rebuild troops, hospital, shields and speedups.
  • Finish remaining collective City-capture rewards.
  • Prepare the Week 7 final battle plan.

Tesla Exclusive Weapon

  • Current calendars place Tesla in Week 6, commonly around Day 4; verify your server timer.
  • Tesla must be 5★ and activation requires 50 dedicated shards.
  • The specific Battle Pass has a short availability window.

🔥 Week 7 — The Final Battle

  • Last major window to convert territory into Season Influence.
  • Prioritize warzone/alliance strategy over personal score.
  • Preserve troops between important fights; monitor hospital and Rescue/Emergency Center if already unlocked on your server wave.
  • Clear remaining missions and rewards before systems begin closing.
  • The Season Store is close to opening: do not waste season currencies or medals.

🎁 Week 8 — contributions, rewards and settlement

  • Settlement phase: rewards reflect season results and contributions.
  • Leadership should distribute alliance rewards before the relevant screens expire.
  • At the end of the week, Season 1-specific buildings and resources disappear.
  • Community guides report conversion of Gene Fragments, Mutant Crystals and Immune Protein into Profession EXP; verify the conversion mail on your server.
  • Exclusive Weapons remain after the season.

Before the final reset

  • Claim every open chest and reward.
  • Check Season Store currencies.
  • Complete final legal captures before territory freezes.
  • Save screenshots of useful rankings and alliance results.
  • Do not assume a seasonal item will survive unless its conversion rule is confirmed.

🎉 After Season 1 — Celebration

The Celebration phase can last several weeks and is very profitable.

  • Black Market: use useful daily refreshes and claim the free item before refreshing again.
  • Bingo Tasks then Black Market Cash Challenge generate Black Market Cash through normal activity.
  • Champion Duel: resync your lineup when your power rises and use prediction mechanics when available.
  • Extra Alliance Duel / VS technologies may appear, increasing points from trucks, secret tasks, survivor recruitment or overall VS scoring.
  • Emergency/Rescue Center may appear here, although newer servers can receive it earlier.

💡 F2P / low-spend strategy

  • Do not try to max everything. Pick one main formation, one EW priority and a profession aligned with your role.
  • Engineer is usually the comfortable growth/efficiency option; War Leader gains value for heavy PvP and rally leaders.
  • An active alliance offsets a large amount of individual power through fragment trades, reinforcements, coordinated captures and collective rewards.
  • Spend for meaningful breakpoints, not for tiny progress across ten systems.

⚠️ Always confirm in-game

  • Exact declaration/war days and times.
  • City and Capitol unlock delays.
  • Influence scoring and ranking rewards.
  • Exact EW Battle Pass windows.
  • End-of-season resource conversion.
